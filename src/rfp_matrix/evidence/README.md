# Evidence producer and change-context APIs

`inventory_report`, `build_matrix`, `assess_requirement`, `revise_requirements` and `reassess_rows` retain their public signatures. Callers inject `provider=create_provider(config, ..., ledger=...)` using the admitted provider-layer factory; the evidence engine does not construct SDKs, read credentials, create ledgers, retry calls or bypass factory admission. No provider means a disabled port for model stages. Method/config/raw-source/corpus binding mismatches fail visibly before judgment. B0a remains deterministic; B0b has typed inventory and deterministic evidence rules; F1 uses typed inventory and matching; F2 uses generated closed labels.

## Closed judgments

`interpret_judgment(outcome, questions, *, method) -> dict` returns `status=ok|labels|unavailable`, preserving the full raw provider result in `raw`. F1/B0b answers must pass the provider's closed distribution validator and selected-confidence consistency. F2 accepts only `needs_review` with `distribution_semantics=generated-closed-labels-no-probabilities`, an exact `raw_classifications` question set, boolean/null Noul labels and registered enum selections. It never converts labels to Noul/Choice probabilities. Raw labels remain review-required. Missing, malformed, refused, budget/deadline/rate-limited or otherwise unavailable stages remain incomplete; no engine retry is attempted. Usage/provenance on unsuccessful responses is retained when JSON-valid, with consumer errors separate from raw outcome status.

Inventory traverses original paragraphs/bullets, resolves literal anchors, preserves independent mandatory/conditional/administrative flags and full rejected-candidate/judgment audit. Explicit structural obligation signals cannot be discarded by a contrary model label; disagreements remain review-required. Unknown requirement labels retain the candidate. The total candidate/audit cap fails visibly rather than dropping context.

Matching offers registered original-coordinate sentence/windows and resolves them before model judgment and again after selection. Support, partial support and counterevidence remain visible. Conflict takes precedence, followed by incomplete/uncalibrated processing, partial evidence, support and absence. Incomplete top-k/window/context, unknown applicability or inventory-method mismatch prevents confident absence/support. No result approves a row. Generated-label evidence is displayed with `needs_review` (or a visible conflict), pending human assessment, never fabricated calibrated confidence. Full raw per-record outcomes preserve model, SDK/configuration, origin, distribution semantics, usage, retries/cache/accounting where supplied; unknown cost stays unknown.

New assessment generation/policy hashes bind the engine policy, exact provider configuration/model/SDK/policies, method, immutable artifact and whole corpus. Manual requirement reassessment preserves requirement versions/anchors, notes, ancestry and retired-row exclusion; approval resets to pending. Source replacement with stale manual anchors fails visibly and needs an explicit application rebase. Local edits cannot silently change methods within an old generation.

## Stale-review context

`describe_changes(old_artifact, new_artifact, old_corpus, new_corpus, *, limit=500, quote_limit=400) -> dict` validates all four immutable inputs and returns a deterministic `context_sha`, source/corpus identities, parser/version/normalization/configuration changes, global stale requirement, page/section deltas, bounded original-coordinate snippets, and complete record added/removed/changed ID lists. Section hashes include original content. Record changes include metadata/terms as well as raw text. Positional page/section comparisons explicitly do not establish ancestry or semantic equivalence. An artifact change leaves ancestry unknown until a separately authorized rebase.

`entries` is globally bounded to `limit` (1..2000), quotes to `quote_limit` (0..2000 codepoints). Counts and omitted-entry count remain visible; `truncated` is not a claim of complete displayed context. `source.changed`, `corpus.changed` and `all_dependent_approvals_stale` depend on exact identities, never displayed-entry truncation or lexical similarity. Every whole-corpus generation change invalidates all dependent approvals even if their cited records are unchanged. This helper explains changes; the review store owns transactional invalidation, persistence, source replacement and CLI/export consumption. No cache or approval may use this summary to optimize away invalidation.

## Amendment correspondence (source only)

`amendment_candidates(old_snapshot, new_snapshot, limit=200)` compares two registered snapshots of the same run. Both immutable artifacts/corpora and active source anchors are validated. Pins include exact raw/artifact/corpus/assessment/matrix/row/requirement identities. Retired historical rows remain visible but cannot become candidate targets; pointers from another historical artifact stay explicitly unresolved. Lexical overlap is a separately labelled proposal, never semantic equivalence. Candidate degree flags expose split/merge ambiguity; dates, conditions and independent attributes remain visible before/after. Candidate limits and incomplete retrieval retain full unmapped mandatory inventory. Added/removed counts mean no lexical proposal found, not established ancestry. No source, row or approval is modified or carried.

Bounds: 2,000 rows/snapshot, 8 million requirement and anchor codepoints, 512 unique lexical tokens/row, 128 rows/token posting, 50,000 pair evaluations, output 1..1,000 candidates. Literal previews are 1,000 codepoints; four anchor previews include 400-character quote_excerpt with quote_excerpt_end/truncation flags and complete original start/end/hash. No geometry or first-substring lookup is invented. Common-token skipping, token/pair bounds and omitted proposals are visible as retrieval_incomplete/truncated. Empty lexical sets are unknown. The final application is responsible for comparing its currently authoritative snapshot with these pinned inputs.

`validate_correspondence_selection(comparison, *, comparison_id, expected_old_identity, expected_new_identity, relation, old_row_revisions, new_row_revisions)` checks exact pins, registered row revisions and one_to_one/split/merge/added/removed cardinality. It is a pure preflight, never authentication, persistence or a concurrency lock. The review store owns internally resolved actor, rationale, request identity, CAS/idempotent append-only events and approval invalidation. New approvals always require separate review.

`web.presentation.amendments_view` / `build_view('amendments', payload)` display plain-text source-linked proposals and separate application-supplied human_events. Human correspondence cannot set a candidate confirmed, populate an approved answer, restore stale approvals or imply compliance. Runtime/tests/security remain unverified.

## Segment disposition and pending promotion

`inventory_audit(artifact, inventory_report)` derives every registered candidate's retained/rejected/uncertain/unattributed disposition from the original report. It verifies exact artifact/policy/registered anchor membership, rejects duplicate/conflicting/outside dispositions, and visibly marks missing attribution. Original structural line segments link to their candidate intervals; multi-atom lines stay uncertain. Table cells without original alignment remain unanchored gaps. Parser/page coverage, typed provider-unavailable and intentional context exclusion remain distinct. `attribution_complete` means accounted structural input only; `semantic_recall` is always not_assessed and all_requirements_found is null. No classifier runs and the machine report is not modified.

`promote_inventory_candidate(artifact, corpus, machine_report, *, candidate_id, assessment_generation, policy, current_rows, interpretation=None, mandatory=None, conditional=None, administrative=None, applicability=None)` prepares one new source-bound pending row. Current rows are REQUIRED for duplicate/limit preflight. Retained machine atoms, existing/retired exact source atoms and unknown/stale pointers are rejected. Independent flag overrides are strict booleans. The current authoritative corpus/policy/generation are supplied by the application; HTTP never supplies arbitrary policies/actors/spans. No provider runs: evidence remains needs_review/incomplete, validation pending, no support/decision/approval. human_origin binds original candidate/report/registry/current policy/corpus and explicitly excludes the row from blind predictions; revision/reassessment preserves that tag. The review store performs final transactional actor/CAS/idempotency/event/row-limit checks and adds the pending row without changing approvals of unchanged rows.

`web.presentation.inventory_audit_view({audit, snapshot, promotions})` supplies closed plain-text entries/tabs, structural gaps/page coverage, current version controls and separately labelled human corrections. Original machine dispositions remain immutable. Missing/stale versions disable eligible choices. `build_view('inventory', payload)` dispatches to it. These APIs were syntax/source-checked only; all runtime/model/parser/DB/browser/tests remain unverified.

## Semantic source-policy upgrades

New artifacts optionally retain policy_provenance with immutable parse configuration and a normalized semantic context. New inventory/corpus/registry/retrieval/assessment/generation hashes bind the actual relevant source context and provider/model/SDK/question policy. These are integrity identities, not execution/quality/authenticity evidence. The provenance layer owns explicit source collection/process pins; constructors/imports do not collect fingerprints or SDKs. Canonical source identity normalizes line endings and semantic AST formatting; method-specific absent SDK metadata never makes the B0a baseline depend on TypeSafe.

source.policy_context(method, parser_profile=None) explicitly invokes the provenance layer's semantic_policy_fingerprint and verifies its closed SEMANTIC_PROFILE descriptor. It preserves full descriptor/status/reasons and emits a normalized context with context_sha and execution_verified:false. source.policy_status(recorded,current) is pure and accepts normalized contexts, never collected-at-the-time status labels as fresh proof. Results are current/missing/legacy/mismatch/restart_required, reasons, action and allows_current_claim. source.artifact_policy_status additionally checks parser/provenance hash/profile; evidence.assessment_policy_status additionally requires the actual current method/provider/model/SDK/question/validation identity (typed current provider missing is missing, not inferred from old answers). All guards are necessary preconditions, never approvals.

The review store collects current contexts on explicit operations and supplies application-computed row/snapshot.policy_status to presenters. Missing/legacy/changed/restart states are visible read-only and cannot expose a current positive/gap answer. Source drift requires safe restart; no watcher, hot reload or automatic live call is installed. Reassess actually recomputes evidence under the selected context; replacing old evidence metadata with a new hash cannot establish a fresh assessment. Local row edits require current/exact policy and keep unrelated row content/CAS intact. Human correction tags remain separate from blind predictions.

Legacy records lacking optional policy_provenance retain exactly the old validated mapping; no default None field is injected into their hashes. SourceRegistry.load, resolve_anchor and resolve_context verify immutable original strings/offsets/digests without executing an unavailable old normalizer. validate_artifact(allow_historical=True) is the explicit integrity-only historical boundary; new normalized lookups remain strict and need reimport for unknown profiles. resolve_evidence(...allow_historical=True) likewise resolves original literal coordinate IDs and hash only, never claims current registered-window semantics. Original snapshots, anchors, decision content revisions and receipt bytes are never rewritten merely by reading history. All runtime/SDK/source-process/legacy activation remains unverified in Dirty Mode.

Stable identity refinement: source.policy_identity(context) contains only semantic profile/method/parser_profile/fingerprint. The provenance descriptor_sha256 and source context_sha protect full descriptor/process-guard record integrity separately. Restart status/reasons are not semantic policy identity; policy-currentness still blocks restart-required even when the stable fingerprint equals a previous process. New artifact policy_profile=m3-artifact-policy-v3, evidence policy/generation-v4 and related corpus/retrieval versions use stable identity. A legacy profile is history, never auto-upgraded. Source-level construction claims remain AST/manual inspection only; neither descriptor collection nor tests ran.
